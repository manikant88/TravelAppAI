# Live meal planning

## Accepted behavior

The live planner asks for one explicit dining preference before searching: vegetarian,
pure-vegetarian restaurants only, non-vegetarian, or both. It also retains allergies,
religious restrictions, intolerances, cuisines, and foods the traveller wants to try as
separate notes or constraints.

Breakfast appears at or near the selected stay from Day 2 onward. Lunch and dinner use
Google Places restaurant candidates and become timed itinerary stops in the same route
chain and map as activities. Regular opening hours must cover the planned meal time when
known. Unknown hours and prices remain explicit.

Google place and text-search evidence does not verify menus, allergens, pure-vegetarian
kitchen operation, or cross-contamination controls.
